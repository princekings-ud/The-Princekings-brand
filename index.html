Client
ZOREVA
Category
Brand Identity
Industry
Luxury Hospitality
Year
2024

ZOREVA was entering a luxury hospitality market where most competitors defaulted to predictable darks and overworked type. The identity needed to command the price point — and the room — before a single guest arrived.

We built a visual system rooted in forest green, chosen deliberately over the expected black. A bespoke interlocking Z mark that carries both motion and heritage. From signage to staff uniform, every touchpoint was designed to signal authority before a word was spoken.

01 — The Logo System

A mark built for permanence

At the core of ZOREVA's identity is a bespoke interlocking Z monogram — a custom form crafted to carry both motion and heritage. No template, no shortcut. The primary logomark pairs the monogram with extended letterforms that signal authority before a single word is read. It works at 16px on a screen and 16 feet on a wall.

02 — Colour & Typography

Chosen deliberately, not by default

ZOREVA's palette is anchored in deep forest green — selected over the predictable darks that dominate luxury hospitality. Gold acts as the accent: warm, earned, and authoritative. The typography follows the same logic: generous spacing, confident weight, built to travel from a business card to a building fascia without losing composure.

03 — Brand in Space

Designed to inhabit real environments

Every mark was tested in three-dimensional context. Signage, wall applications, and dimensional lettering were all developed to ensure the identity maintains its weight at architectural scale — not just in a presentation PDF. The green-and-gold palette commands the room before a guest sits down.

04 — Applications & Collateral

Every touchpoint, considered

A brand identity that only lives on a logo sheet is incomplete. For ZOREVA, every guest-facing surface was designed — from menus and business cards to staff uniform and packaging. The system maintains its elegance whether held in a guest's hand or mounted above an entrance.

05 — Outdoor & Final Identity

The brand travels beyond the building

ZOREVA's identity was built to extend into the city. Outdoor applications — from billboard to vehicle branding — were designed to carry the same authority as the interior. The final brand system is cohesive across every format: print, digital, environmental, and everything in between.

Asili

Brand Identity

Lifestyle & Culture
Year
2024

Asili — meaning origin, authentic, true to source — needed a visual identity that matched the weight of that name. The brand sits at the intersection of African heritage and contemporary lifestyle, and required a system that could carry both without compromise.

We built an identity rooted in cultural confidence: bold enough to stand on the shelf, refined enough to sit in premium spaces. Every design decision traces back to the core idea — authentic, from the source.

01 — The Logo System

A mark that carries the name

The Asili logomark was built to communicate authenticity before a single word is read. Clean, intentional, and grounded — the mark draws on the idea of origin without resorting to cliché. It scales from a product label to a storefront without losing its voice.

02 — Colour & Typography

A palette with a point of view

Asili's colour system was chosen to feel warm, grounded, and premium simultaneously — earthy tones that speak to African landscape and heritage, lifted by refined typographic choices that position the brand for contemporary, premium spaces. Nothing arbitrary. Every choice earns its place.

03 — Brand in Context

Identity that lives in real spaces

Brand identity only proves itself when it leaves the design file. We tested Asili's system across the environments it would actually inhabit — product surfaces, retail contexts, digital spaces — to ensure the identity holds its authority at every scale and format.

04 — Brand Applications

Every touchpoint, considered

From packaging to promotional materials, every surface was designed as an extension of the Asili identity — not an afterthought. The system was built to maintain its character whether it's in a customer's hands, on a shelf, or across digital channels.

05 — The Complete System

A brand built for the long run

The final Asili identity system is consistent, adaptable, and built to grow with the brand. Every element — from the mark to the colour palette to the type choices — works together as a unified whole. This is a brand that knows exactly who it is.
